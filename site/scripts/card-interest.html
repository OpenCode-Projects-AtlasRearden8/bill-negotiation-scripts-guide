Card-interest rate script (one page)

Read aloud at normal speed. Pauses in brackets are real.

Before dialing

  • Honest facts check (know these cold):
  • Years as cardholder: ______ (true number only)
  • Payment history: ______ (never missed / one late in [year] / etc.)
  • Current APR: % · Balance: $
  • Comparable real offers: ______% (cards you'd actually qualify for)
  • Walk-away decision: if they say no, do you keep paying on schedule? Usually the answer is yes — say so. That's okay. The ask costs nothing.

The call

Open:

"Hi, I'm [Your Name], card ending in [last 4]. Quick, friendly request: I'd like to ask for a reduced interest rate. Is that something you can look at today, or is it the wrong line? You can just tell me — no worries either way."

The honest ask (after they confirm they're the right person):

"I've been a cardholder since [year] and I've kept my payments [current / never missed / caught back up since X]. I'm carrying a balance of about [$] at [APR]%, and at this rate most of my payment is interest. I'd like to stay with this card — could you lower the rate, ideally closer to [comparable offer]%?"

[Pause. One common reply: "Did you know your credit score…?" They can do a soft pull. It's their choice and their tool — you're allowed to let them.]

If they decline — hear the reason, leave the door open:

"Okay, I appreciate you telling me why. How long before it's reasonable to ask again — ninety days? Six months? I'll keep paying as agreed meanwhile, and I may call back around [date]."

If a partial cut is offered:

"That's real movement, thank you. Can you confirm exactly what APR goes with which portion of the balance — is it everything, or new purchases only?"

If they mention a transfer/balance-promo instead:

"What are the transfer fee and the promo end date? And does the regular rate apply to old purchases meanwhile? I'd like the full picture before deciding."

Closing — read back every promise:

"To be sure I heard it: [new APR]% on [portion], effective [date], no fee for the change, remaining terms unchanged. Can I get that in writing through my online messages with a reference number?"

Warm exit:

"Thank you for actually looking. Whether it's yes or no, I appreciate the time."

After the call

  • Promise tracker: date, rep name, reason given, promised APR + date, rep's reference number. Set a calendar reminder for the "ask again by" date.
  • Watch the next statement. Empty promises evaporate — written ones stick. If the rate never moved → escalation ladder, level 2, notes in hand.
