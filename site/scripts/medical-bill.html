Medical-bill script (one page)

Read aloud at normal speed. Kindness is not weakness here — it's strategy.

Before dialing

  • Know your two numbers: total billed $______ · monthly you can honestly afford $______ (from walk-away math).
  • Gather, don't surrender: the itemized statement and your insurance explanation of benefits (EOB). You ask for an itemized bill if you have only a summary — that's normal and expected.
  • Ask first about financial assistance / charity care. Most nonprofit hospitals have one. It is not charity for you; it's a program you may be entitled to.

The call

Open:

"Hi, I'm [Your Name]. I'm calling about a bill from [facility], dated [date], in the amount of [$total]. I intend to pay for the care I received — I'm calling because the full amount doesn't fit my budget, and I want to work with you on a fair arrangement. Two minutes?"

Path A — financial assistance (start here):

"Does [facility] have a financial-assistance or charity-care policy, and could you mail or email me the application? I want to find out whether I qualify before setting up anything else."

Path B — itemized bill & questions:

"What I have is a summary. Could I get an itemized bill for this account? And can you help me understand charges [name any line] — I can't cancel past care, of course, but I'd like to be able to check it against my EOB."

Path C — discount + plan (if assistance doesn't apply):

"If I set up monthly payments of [$affordable], is there a discount for paying that consistently — or a prompt-pay or self-pay rate that would reduce the balance?"

Possible follow: "If a lump sum of [$x] settled this today, would that be less than the full balance? I'm not promising — I'd want the number in writing before deciding."

Saying no kindly (they will offer bigger payments):

"I hear you, and I wish I could commit to [$bigger]. Honestly, [$afford- able] is what my budget carries — if I promise more, I'd be at risk of missing a payment later, and I don't think either of us wants that. Can we make [$affordable] official?"

Closing — read back every promise:

"Confirming: [assistance application starts a payment freeze — yes? / balance reduced to $___ / plan of $___ monthly starting ___ / any late fees already on the account waived]. Could you email a confirmation and the account reference number?"

Warm exit:

"Thank you for working with me on this. I'll watch for the email."

After the call

  • Promise tracker: date, rep name, promises, due dates, reference numbers.
  • Never read your full account number, SSN, or card details into this kind of call from memory while flustered — check statements, and never if the hospital called you. You call them on the number printed on your statement.
  • If the promised letter never arrives → escalation ladder, level 2.
