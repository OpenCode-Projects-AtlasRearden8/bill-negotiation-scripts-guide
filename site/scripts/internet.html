Internet provider script (one page)

Read aloud at normal speed. Pauses in brackets are real — let them breathe.

Before dialing

  • Walk-away math done? Target: $______ · Ceiling: $______ · Walk-away: $______
  • Leverage (true things only): customer since ______ · last bill $______ · real competitor offer $______ for ______
  • Call on a weekday morning if you can. You'll get longer attention.

The call

Gatekeeper greeting:

"Hi! My name is [Your Name], account ending in [last 4 digits]. I have a billing question and I think you're the right person to help. Do you have two minutes?"

The honest setup:

"I've been a customer since [year], and I'd like to stay. But my bill went up from [old $] to [new $], and my budget genuinely can't stretch to that. Before I look at other options, I wanted to ask you first — is there anything you can do?"

[Pause. Let them go first. They often have something on the screen already.]

If they offer a small discount:

"That helps, thank you. Is that the best you have, or is there a retention plan I should be asking about? I'd rather get it right in one call than make you do this again next month."

If they say there's nothing available:

"Understood, and I appreciate the straight answer. [Competitor] is advertising [speed] for [$] — I can send you the link if that's useful. Is there a loyalty rate or a one-cycle credit you can promise, or should I prepare to switch?"

If an add-on is dangled ("I can knock $10 off if you add…"):

"Thank you, but no — adding a service to save money isn't the math I need. Can we get to [$target] without adding anything?"

Closing — read back every promise:

"Just so I have it right: [summarize each promise — dollar amount, discount name, which bill it appears on, for how long]. Does that match what you've entered? Could you email me a confirmation with a reference number?"

Warm exit (any outcome):

"I really appreciate you being straight with me. I'll look for that confirmation. Have a good one."

After the call

  • Promise tracker: date, rep name, every promise, due date. (30 seconds.)
  • If no confirmation email in 24h → escalation ladder, level 2.
