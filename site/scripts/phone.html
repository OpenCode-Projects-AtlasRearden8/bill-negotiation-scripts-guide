Phone carrier script (one page)

Read aloud at normal speed. Pauses in brackets are real.

Before dialing

  • Walk-away math done? Target: $______ · Ceiling: $______ · Walk-away: $______
  • Leverage (true things only): tenure ______ · current plan $______ · real competitor quote $______ · device paid off? yes / no
  • Know your data pattern honestly (heavy user? mostly Wi-Fi?). "My usage" beats "the price" with carriers.

The call

Open:

"Hi, I'm [Your Name], mobile account ending in [last 4]. I want to lower my monthly bill, and I'm hoping you can walk me through my options. Roughly two minutes of your time?"

The honest ask:

"I've been with you since [year] and I'd honestly like to stay. My bill is [new $] a month and my budget can't carry that. Are there current plans or loyalty rates that would give me the same coverage closer to [target $]?"

[Pause. They will usually offer a plan tour. Let them — then redirect:]

"I don't need more data or perks — usage is about as it's always been. What I need is the same service closer to [$target]. Which of today's plans actually gets me there?"

If device installment is mentioned:

"Right — and to be clear, I'm asking about the plan line, not the device line. If my device is paid off [or: once it is], what's plan price alone?"

If they push a 'free phone' upgrade (classic trojan horse):

"That's generous, but a new device raises the monthly total — that's the opposite of what I called for. Kindly no. Same question: what's the best rate for keeping everything as it is?"

If nothing's available:

"Appreciate the honesty. [Competitor] quoted me [speed/coverage] at [$]. If you can't get near that, I'll start a port-out in [timeframe]. If something changes on your side, I'd happily take the call back."

Closing — read back every promise:

"Confirming: [each promise: price, plan name, taxes-in or not, contract end date, any fee]. Will you text or email that confirmation with a reference number?"

Warm exit:

"You made this easy — thank you. Have a good one."

After the call

  • Promise tracker: date, rep name, promises, due dates. Proration note! Plan changes often bill on a half-cycle. Expect a weird first bill.
  • Watch the next statement like a hawk. If the promise didn't happen → escalation ladder, level 2, with your notes.
