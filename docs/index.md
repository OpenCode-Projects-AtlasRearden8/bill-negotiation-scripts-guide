# Calmer Bills — start here

A ring-binder of phone-call scripts for the five conversations most of us
avoid: internet, phone, insurance, card interest, and medical bills.
Everything is honest leverage, kind words, written receipts, and math you
do before you dial — never pressure, never bluffs.

> **Education only — never legal, financial, or medical advice.**
> This binder explains how conversations usually go. It doesn't replace
> reading your own statements or a licensed professional for your case.

## The binder in four sentences

Do the math first, so their tone doesn't do it for you. Say only true
things. Get every promise in your tracker and in writing. Stay kind —
especially when escalating — because warmth with receipts wins.
