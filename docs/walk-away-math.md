# Walk-away math (do this before you dial)

Ten quiet minutes with a calculator will do more for this phone call than
any script. The point isn't doom — it's that *you* pick the number where
the deal is good enough, so the rep's tone never picks it for you.

## Step 1 — Your true budget line

Write down the single most you can pay per month **without borrowing from a
friend, a card, or a future you**. Not the "maybe if things go well" number.
The honest one.

```text
Comfortable monthly ceiling:  $ ________
```

## Step 2 — The deal you'd sign today

If someone offered you this price right now, would you say "done" without
hesitating? That's your target.

```text
Target price/plan:            $ ________ per month
```

## Step 3 — The walk-away point

The lowest number where accepting still feels calm — no dread, no regret
tomorrow morning. If the best offer on the table is *worse* than this, you
thank them kindly and hang up. Clue: this is usually a round number you can
explain in one breath.

```text
Walk-away point:              $ ________ per month
```

> If Steps 1–3 conflict (target below ceiling but above walk-away), trust
> the walk-away point. It's the one you'll be living with.

## Step 4 — Honest leverage inventory

Only things that are *actually true* get to go on this list. Each one is a
legitimate, kind thing to mention:

- [ ] I have been a customer since ______ (years).
- [ ] My bill rose from $____ to $____ this cycle.
- [ ] A competing real offer: $______ for ______ (I can send it if asked).
- [ ] My budget genuinely cannot stretch past $______ (hardship, plain words).
- [ ] I am calling for patience, not for a fight — I'd rather stay with you.

## Step 5 — Your exit line, written ahead of time

Decide the sentence you'll say when it's over, whichever way it goes:

- **Accepted:** "Great — send me the confirmation in writing, please."
- **Walked away:** "I appreciate you listening. I'll take a week to think
  about it and I may call back. Thank you."

Notice the walk-away version doesn't threaten, slam, or cancel on the spot.
You're allowed a week. The door stays open, your dignity stays intact, and
9 times out of 10 a callback gets a better offer routed to it.

---

One page done. Now open the script for today's call and breathe easy —
you already know your numbers.
